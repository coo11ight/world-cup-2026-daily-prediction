# Source Checklist / 来源清单

Use current links and cite access time. Availability varies by country; when a source is blocked or stale, use a comparable reputable source and say what changed.

## Fixtures, Results, Squads / 赛程、赛果、阵容

- FIFA tournament hub and match centre: `https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026`
- FIFA fixtures/schedule pages and official PDFs when available.
- Official national team federation sites and verified social accounts for squad, injury, suspension, and lineup announcements.
- Major sports desks for cross-checking: ESPN, BBC Sport, The Athletic, Reuters, AP, Sky Sports.

## Odds And Markets / 赔率与盘口

Use at least two sources when possible:

- Odds aggregators: OddsPortal, Oddschecker, Action Network, Covers, VegasInsider.
- Sportsbooks where legally accessible: bet365, DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, Pinnacle, William Hill, Unibet.
- Exchanges where accessible: Betfair Exchange, Smarkets.

Record:

- Market type: 1X2, draw no bet, double chance, over/under, both teams to score, to qualify, outright.
- Odds format: decimal, fractional, American.
- Timestamp and timezone.
- Whether odds are pre-match or live.
- If odds are unavailable, use ratings/form only and lower confidence.

## Ratings And Strength / 球队实力评分

- FIFA Men's Ranking: `https://inside.fifa.com/fifa-world-ranking/men`
- World Football Elo Ratings: `https://eloratings.net/`
- Opta/Analyst power rankings or team-strength articles when available.
- Transfermarkt squad value and player availability can be context, but do not treat market value as a standalone predictor.
- Stats providers such as FBref, FotMob, SofaScore, Statbunker, or Soccerway can support form and performance checks.

## Recent Form / 近期战绩

Prefer the latest 5-10 matches, separated by match type:

- World Cup matches and qualifiers.
- Continental tournaments and Nations League/official competitions.
- Friendlies, weighted lower.

Capture opponent strength, venue, scoreline, xG or shot quality if available, red cards, and major lineup absences.

## Context / 比赛背景

Check only when material:

- Venue, travel, rest days, kickoff time, weather, altitude, and pitch condition.
- Group table incentives, rotation risk, suspension risk, and knockout extra-time/penalty rules.
- Host or near-home advantage.

## Conflict Handling / 来源冲突

- Fixtures/results: prefer FIFA or tournament official source.
- Squad/lineups: prefer team/FIFA official; otherwise cite multiple reputable reporters.
- Odds: report a range or consensus; do not average stale and live prices.
- Ratings: report source-specific differences instead of forcing agreement.
